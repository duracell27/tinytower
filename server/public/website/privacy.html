Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Vibe Tower ("we," "our," or "us") is developed and operated by Volodymyr Shmidt. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Vibe Tower mobile application ("the App"), how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

When you create an account and use the App, we collect:

  • Account information — your email address and username when you register with email, or your Google account name and email when you sign in with Google.
  • Game data — your tower progress, unlocked floors, coin and gem balances, purchases, achievements, tournament history, and gameplay statistics.
  • Device information — device model, operating system version, and app version, used for troubleshooting and compatibility.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To sync your game progress across devices and sessions.
  • To authenticate your identity securely (Google OAuth, email + password).
  • To send transactional emails — account confirmation, password reset links, and welcome messages.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To operate tournament and leaderboard features that compare scores across players.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We do not serve third-party advertising.
  • We do not share your information with anyone except the infrastructure services required to run the App (see below).

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the App. Each has its own privacy policy governing data it processes.

  • Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0) — used as an optional authentication method. Google's privacy policy applies to the sign-in flow.
  • Resend — used to deliver transactional emails (password reset, welcome). Only your email address is shared for delivery.
  • Sentry — used for crash reporting and error monitoring. Sentry may receive error details, device information, and anonymized user identifiers to help us diagnose and fix issues. See sentry.io/privacy.
  • Cloud infrastructure — our server, database (PostgreSQL), and cache (Redis) are hosted on secure cloud infrastructure. No personal data is shared with the provider beyond what is needed to run the service.

Data Retention

Your account data and game progress are retained for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account or request deletion, we remove your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to restrict how we process it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and short-lived authentication tokens stored in Redis. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
